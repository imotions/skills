# Articles

## Eye tracking
- [The fundamentals of eye tracking part 1: The link between theory and research question](./articles/fundamentals_of_eye_tracking_part_1.md) [(Source)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/s13428-024-02544-8) Gives examples of how eye-tracking can be used in different application areas, especially within research on oculomotor control, reading, scene perception, visually guided task execution, expertise, and instructional design.
- [The fundamentals of eye tracking part 2: From research question to operationalization](./articles/fundamentals_of_eye_tracking_part_2.md) [(Source)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/s13428-024-02590-2) Gives overview of common measures in eye tracking (e.g. fixations and saccades, pupil size, blinks, AOIs) and how eye tracking measures can be used as a proxy to answer research question through operationalizion.
- [Minimal reporting guideline for research involving eye tracking (2023 edition)](./articles/minimal_reporting_guideline_eye_tracking_2023.md) [(Source)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/s13428-023-02187-1) Discusses a checklist of what to include when writing up eye tracking results in an Academic context.
- [The area-of-interest problem in eyetracking research: A noise-robust solution for face and sparse stimuli](./articles/area_of_interest_problem_eyetracking.md) [(Source)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/s13428-015-0676-y) Discusses what one should consider when defining Areas of Interest and what to consider when the data is noisy. 
- [iMotions WebET 3.0](./articles/WebET3.0_accuracy_whitepaper_March_2023.md) [(Source)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/372236593_Webcam_Based_Eye_Tracking_-Whitepaper_v3) A comprehensive overview of the webcam-based eye tracking algorithm used for iMotions Online studies.

## Facial expressions
- [Facial Expression Analysis - The Complete Pocket Guide](./articles/Facial_Expression_Analysis_Complete_Pocket_Guide.md) A reference covering the scientific foundations, methods, study design, data processing, interpretation, and multimodal use of facial expression analysis, including fEMG, FACS, automatic facial coding, baselining, thresholding, aggregation, and practical limitations.
- [AI-Driven Emotion Recognition 101: All About Emotion Detection and Affectiva’s Emotion Metrics](./articles/ai-driven-emotion-recognition.md) [(Source)](https://imotions.com/blog/insights/research-insights/ai-driven-emotion-recognition/) Explains how Affectiva's AI maps facial expressions to nine emotions and how engagement and valence metrics are calculated.
